Privacy

Noema collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics in the app, no advertising and no tracking.

The Mac app

The app reads public pages and keeps nothing:

  • The lab's ledger and list of reported results, from github.com.
  • The status of the lab's sequence edits, from oeis.org.
  • The results tables the lab reported, from clinicaltrials.gov.

Those requests carry no identifier of yours. They are made when the app opens and when you press Check now. Nothing is written to disk beyond what macOS caches for any network request.

This website

The site is served by Cloudflare, which counts page views without cookies. No form on the site collects personal information.
